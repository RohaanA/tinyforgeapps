Niwala Privacy Policy

Niwala: Calorie Counter & Diet, by TinyForgeApps · Effective 30 September 2026

In short
  • Niwala has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The camera form coach works entirely on your phone. Video is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • You can delete all your data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Niwala is made by TinyForgeApps, an independent developer. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email rohaanpk3@gmail.com.

What Niwala stores, and where

To work, Niwala keeps the following information only on your device, in the app's private storage. We (TinyForgeApps) never receive it.

InformationWhy the app uses it
Profile: sex, age, height, current and goal weight, activity level, unitsTo calculate your calorie and protein targets and your plan
Food log, custom foods, favouritesTo track calories and nutrients and show your progress
Weigh-insTo show your weight trend and, if you choose, estimate your real calorie needs
Workouts, saved routines, programs and challengesTo track exercise and show your progress
Settings (theme, sounds, reminders, Gol's outfit, optional creator name)To remember your preferences

Because this information never leaves your phone, uninstalling Niwala or using Plan → Delete all my data removes it completely.

Camera

If you use the camera form coach, Niwala asks for camera permission. The camera image is analysed on your phone by a pose-detection model to count reps and check your form. Video and images are never recorded, stored or transmitted. The camera is only used while the coach screen is open.

Notifications

If you turn on meal reminders or movement breaks, Niwala schedules notifications on your phone. They are created by the app on your device; no notification service receives your data. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Things you choose to share

Niwala only sends information off your phone when you tap a share button, and only what that button describes:

  • Share cards and weekly summaries: an image showing the stats on the card.
  • Nudges, workouts and challenges: a text message containing the nudge, the workout or challenge, a code to import it, and a link to Niwala on Google Play. Shared workouts can include the creator name you entered.
  • Backups: a file with all your Niwala data, sent to wherever you choose (for example Google Drive or email).

These are sent through your phone's share sheet or WhatsApp, to people or places you pick. The apps you share with handle that content under their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is required.
  • No advertising, and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No sale or sharing of personal or health data with anyone.
  • No location access.

Health information

Niwala is a general wellness app for adults. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Calorie values and targets are estimates. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet or exercise if you have a health condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take medication.

Niwala does not currently connect to Health Connect or other health platforms. If a future version adds this, it will ask for your permission first, read or write only the data types it explains, keep that data on your phone, and this policy will be updated before the feature is released.

Children

Niwala is intended for adults 18 and over, and the app does not create plans for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your phone's own security (such as its screen lock and encryption). Backup files you export are not encrypted by Niwala, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Your choices and rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your device: edit or remove entries in the app, delete everything under Plan → Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes, such as any new way data leaves your phone, will also be explained in the app before they take effect.
