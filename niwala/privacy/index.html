Niwala Privacy Policy

Niwala, by TinyForgeApps · Last updated 21 October 2026

In short
  • Niwala has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal or health data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • If you turn on sync, Niwala reads your steps and sleep from Health Connect or Apple Health. It never writes to them and reads nothing else.
  • The camera and GPS are only used while you use the feature, and what they capture stays on your phone.
  • You can delete all your data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Niwala is made by TinyForgeApps, an independent developer. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email rohaanpk3@gmail.com.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you turn on sync in Settings, Niwala asks for permission to read two kinds of data: your daily step count (Steps) and your sleep sessions (Sleep). It does not write to Health Connect or Apple Health and does not read any other data type.

  • Steps are shown on the Food tab, the home screen widget and your weekly recap.
  • Sleep is shown on the Sleep screen and feeds your readiness score.

This data is read on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used for credit, insurance or employment decisions. Niwala's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can stop sync in the app at any time, or remove Niwala's access in Health Connect or the Health app.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: for the form coach, progress photos and food barcode scanning. The camera image is analysed on your phone. Video is never recorded, stored or transmitted, and the camera is only used while that screen is open.
  • Location: only while you track a run, walk or ride that you started, to map and measure it. Niwala does not use location in the background when you are not tracking, and your route stays on your phone.
  • Physical activity: to count steps with your phone's own sensor when health sync is off.
  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on. They are created on your device; no notification service receives your data.
  • Photos: to save the cards you make to your gallery, or pick a progress photo.

When information leaves your phone

  • Barcode lookups: when you scan a packaged food, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to get its nutrition information.
  • Share cards and weekly summaries: an image showing the stats on the card. Cards never show your weight or cycle.
  • Nudges, workouts and challenges: a text message with the nudge, workout or challenge, a code to import it, and a link to Niwala on Google Play. Shared workouts can include the creator name you entered.
  • Backups and spreadsheet exports: a file with your Niwala data (without cycle data), sent to wherever you choose, for example Google Drive or email.

Apart from barcode lookups, these only happen when you tap a share or backup button. They go through your phone's share sheet or WhatsApp, and the apps you share with handle that content under their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is required.
  • No advertising, and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No sale or sharing of personal or health data with anyone.
  • No background location.

Health information

Niwala is a general wellness app for adults. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Calorie values, readiness scores, workout suggestions, injury swaps and cycle predictions are estimates and general guidance. Cycle predictions are not birth control. Talk to a doctor or other qualified health professional for medical advice, and before changing your diet or exercise if you have a health condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take medication.

Children

Niwala is intended for adults 18 and over, and the app does not create plans for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your phone's own security (such as its screen lock and encryption). Backup files you export are not encrypted by Niwala, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Your choices and deleting your data

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your device: edit or remove entries in the app, delete everything under Plan → Settings → Delete all my data, delete just your cycle log under Plan → Settings → Delete my cycle log, or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes, such as any new way data leaves your phone, will also be explained in the app before they take effect.
